# RHODES Framework

## Name
Role-How-Objectives-Details-Examples-Style

## Structure
- **Role**: Role
- **How**: Method/approach
- **Objectives**: Goals
- **Details**: Details
- **Examples**: Examples
- **Style**: Style

## Use Cases
- Creative writing
- Marketing content creation
- Projects with strict style and tone requirements
- Tasks requiring highly customized output
- Creative planning

## Example
**Input**: "Write a luxury goods advertisement copy"

**Optimized**:
```
Role:
You are a luxury goods advertising copywriter with 20 years of experience, having served top brands such as Rolex, Hermès, and Cartier. You have a deep understanding of luxury consumer psychology and excel at conveying prestige and elegance through concise yet powerful language.

How:
- Use implication rather than direct description
- Emphasize heritage and craftsmanship
- Evoke emotional resonance
- Create a sense of scarcity and exclusivity
- Avoid words like "cheap" or "cost-effective"

Objectives:
- Create advertising copy for a new handcrafted timepiece
- Target audience: successful men aged 35-50, annual income 1M+
- Convey: craftsmanship, heritage, uniqueness
- Length: 50-80 words
- Channel: full-page magazine advertisement

Details:

Product information:
- Name: Heritage 1924
- Features: Fully handcrafted, limited to 100 pieces
- Craftsmanship: Hand-assembled by Swiss watchmakers, each piece taking 6 months
- Materials: 18K rose gold, crocodile leather strap
- Price: 280,000 yuan

Brand tone:
- Keywords: heritage, craftsmanship, time, eternity
- Avoid: technological feel, youthful, sporty
- Atmosphere: classic, elegant, understated luxury

Examples:

Reference 1 (Rolex):
"A crown for every achievement."
Characteristics: concise, double meaning, prestige

Reference 2 (Patek Philippe):
"You never actually own a Patek Philippe. You merely look after it for the next generation."
Characteristics: heritage, emotion, eternity

Reference 3 (Hermès):
"Crafted for the extraordinary."
Characteristics: craftsmanship, uniqueness, simplicity

Style:

Language style:
- Sentence structure: primarily short sentences with rhythm
- Vocabulary: elegant, classical, avoid colloquialisms
- Rhetoric: moderate use of metaphor and symbolism
- Person: second person, direct dialogue

Visual style (copy layout):
- Main headline: large serif font
- Subtitle: small, supplementary explanation
- Whitespace: generous whitespace to convey high-end feel
- Position: usually placed near the visual focal point

Complete copy options:

Option 1:
"Six months. One master. A lifetime of moments.
Heritage 1924 — for those who understand that true luxury is time itself."

Option 2:
"Not all legacies are inherited. Some are crafted.
Heritage 1924 — 100 pieces. 100 stories."

Option 3:
"Time is the ultimate luxury. We treat it with reverence.
Heritage 1924 — where craftsmanship meets eternity."

Recommendation:
Option 2 is recommended, because:
- "Not all legacies are inherited" breaks conventional thinking and arouses curiosity
- "Crafted" emphasizes craftsmanship
- "100 pieces. 100 stories" is concise and powerful, emphasizing scarcity
```

## Usage Tips
- The role should be professional and authoritative
- The method should follow industry best practices
- Details determine quality
- Examples provide reference direction
- Style should be consistent throughout
